模糊测试

模糊测试（Fuzzing）是一种通过向程序提供大量非预期、随机或半随机的输入，并监控程序是否出现崩溃、断言失败或内存泄漏等异常，从而发现软件错误和安全漏洞的自动化测试技术。
  1. Cheng Shi, Jiongchi Yu, Ziming Zhao, Jiongyi Chen, Fan Zhang*, "CGIFuzz: Enabling Gray-Box Fuzzing for Web CGI of IoT Devices," IEEE Transactions on Information Forensics and Security (TIFS), Vol.20, pp.10921 - 10936, 2025. [PDF]
  2. Xuechao Du, Andong Chen, Boyuan He, Hao Chen, Fan Zhang, Yan Chen, "AFLIoT: Fuzzing on Linux-based IoT Device with Binary-level Instrumentation," Computers & Security (COSE), Volume 122, Issue C, 122: 102889, 2023. [PDF]
固件仿真

固件仿真是指在脱离真实物理硬件环境的情况下，利用虚拟化或模拟技术在计算机上运行和动态调试物联网设备（如路由器、摄像头等）固件程序的技术。
  1. Yanbing Shen, Hao Sun, Jiacheng Wang, Haitao Xu, Gang Liu, Fan Zhang, "HoneySentry: A High-Fidelity Interactive IoT Honeypot for Advanced Threat Detection," Proceedings of 28th International Conference on Information Security (ISC) , pp.347-366, 2025. [PDF]
