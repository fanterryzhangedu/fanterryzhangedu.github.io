成员推断攻击是机器学习算法面临的安全威胁之一,用于推断特定数据是否存在于机器学习模型的训练集中。

对抗样本攻击

对抗样本就是指在原始样本添加一些人眼无法察觉的扰动（这样的扰动不会影响人类的识别，但是却很容易欺骗模型），致使机器做出错误的判断。
  1. Ziming Zhao, Zhaoxuan Li, Tingting Li, Fan Zhang*, "Assessing and Improving DNN Robustness Against Adversarial Examples From the Perspective of Fully Connected Layers," IEEE Transactions on Information Forensics and Security (TIFS), Vol.21, pp.3781-3796, 2026. [PDF]
  2. Ziming Zhao, Zhaoxuan Li, Tingting Li, Fan Zhang*, "Stealthy-AE: Generating Stealthy Adversarial Examples through Online Social Networks," Proceedings of the 33rd ACM International Conference on Multimedi (MM), pp.3923-3931, 2025. [PDF]
  3. Ziming Zhao, Zhaoxuan Li, Fan Zhang*, Ziqi Yang, Shuang Luo, Tingting Li, Rui Zhang, Kui Ren, "SAGE: Steering the Adversarial Generation of Examples With Accelerations," IEEE Transactions on Information Forensics and Security (TIFS), Vol.18, pp.789-803, 2023. [PDF]
