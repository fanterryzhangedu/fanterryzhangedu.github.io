密码故障攻击：故障注入和故障分析

密码故障攻击（Fault Attack，简称FA）是一种利用电压毛刺、时钟毛刺、激光干扰等方式迫使密码模块运算产生错误，并通过对错误结果的分析进行密钥恢复的技术。密码故障攻击包含两个阶段：故障注入（Fault Injection）和故障分析（Fault Analysis）。
  1. Xue Gong, Xin Zhang, Qianmei Wu, Fan Zhang*, Junge Xu, Qingni Shen, Zhi Zhang, "Practical Opcode-based Fault Attack on AES-NI," IACR Transactions on Cryptographic Hardware and Embedded Systems (TCHES), Vol.2025, No.3, pp.693-716, 2025. [PDF]
  2. Fan Zhang*, Yiran Zhang, Huilong Jiang, Xiang Zhu, Shivam Bhasin, Xinjie Zhao, Zhe Liu, Dawu Gu, Kui Ren "Persistent Fault Attack in Practice," IACR Transactions on Cryptographic Hardware and Embedded Systems (TCHES), Vol.2020, No.2, pp.172-195, 2020.[PDF]
  3. Fan Zhang*, Xiaoxuan Lou, Xinjie Zhao, Shivam Bhasin, Wei He, Ruyi Ding, Samiya Qureshi, Kui Ren, "Persistent Fault Analysis on Block Ciphers," IACR Transactions on Cryptographic Hardware and Embedded Systems (TCHES), Vol.2018, No.3, pp.150-172, 2018.[PDF]
密码旁路攻击：泄露采集和旁路分析

密码旁路攻击（Side Channel Attack 简称SCA），又称侧信道攻击，核心思想是通过加密软件或硬件运行时产生的各种功耗、电磁等泄漏信息，并结合密码算法的相关结构信息，来恢复最终的密钥。密码旁路攻击包含两个阶段：旁路泄露采集（Leakage Collection）和旁路分析（Side Channel Analysis）。
  1. Qianmei Wu, Wei Cheng*, Sylvain Guilley, Fan Zhang*, Wei Fu, "On Efficient and Secure Code-based Masking: A Pragmatic Evaluation," IACR Transactions on Cryptographic Hardware and Embedded Systems (TCHES), Vol.2022, No.3, pp.192-222, 2022. [PDF]
  2. Fan Zhang*, Zhiyong Wang, Haoting Shen, Bolin Yang, Qianmei Wu, Kui Ren, "DARPT: Defense against Remote Physical Attack based on TDC in Multi-tenant Scenario," Proceedings of the 59th ACM/EDAC/IEEE Design Automation Conferenc (DAC), pp.559-564, 2022.[PDF]
  3. Fan Zhang*, Bin Shao, Guorui Xu, Bolin Yang, Ziqi Yang, Zhan Qin, Kui Ren, "From Homogeneous to Heterogeneous: Leveraging Deep Learning based Power Analysis across Devices," Proceedings of the 57th ACM/EDAC/IEEE Design Automation Conferenc (DAC), pp.1-6, 2020.[PDF]
  4. Xinjie Zhao, Fan Zhang, Tao Wang, Shize Guo, Zhijie Jerry Shi, Huiying Liu, Keke Ji. "MDASCA: An Enhanced Algebraic Side-Channel Attack for Error Tolerance and New Leakage Model Exploitation," In Constructive Side-Channel Analysis and Secure Design (COSADE) , LNCS, vol. 7275, pp. 231-248, 2012.  (Best Paper Award)[PDF]
神经网络故障攻击：故障注入和分类出错

针对神经网络的故障攻击利用电压毛刺、时钟毛刺、激光干扰等方式迫使神经网络底层硬件在计算机过程中出现异常，并最终导致推理分类的结果出现错误。

  3. Wenye Liu, Chip-Hong Chang, Fan Zhang, Xiaoxuan Lou, "Imperceptible Misclassification Attack on Deep Learning Accelerator by Glitch Injection," Proceedings of the 57th ACM/EDAC/IEEE Design Automation Conferenc (DAC), pp.1-6, 2020.[PDF]
