To those who are interested to join my group as Ph.D. or Postdoc

Please carefully check two sources: my personal website and my laboratory website to see if you can get enough information. If not (parts of pages under construction, sorry), please directly contact me for more inforamtion.

I am working on various topics of general security, including: side channel analysis and countermeasures, cryptography, hardware security, computer architecture, system security, network securirty. Please carefully read my publications to see if you are interested on my current research. Recenly I am working on combining SCA with other research areas such as binary analysis, network, and imporiving its applicability in practice. I am also setting a lof of new topics in IoT security and Industrial security.

Those who are interested in working on genenral security, and would like to enjoy an amazing academic life in the beautiful city of HangZhou and explore new adventures in the rising China, please send me the following documents if you can provide.

  • Undergraduate and graduate transcripts (required)
  • A full CV (required)
  • PDF version of your published papers if you have (optional)
  • A short letter stating why you are interested and what is your plan (optional)
