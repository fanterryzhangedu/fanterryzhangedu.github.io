To those who are interested to get the financial support

You are always welcome to apply for the Ph.D. or PostDoc positions in my group. There are two possible sources to apply for the financail aids to support your academic life in Zhejiang University.

One is the China Scholarship Council, in short CSC, which normally provides four to six years of scholarship to those who come from oversea and puruse Ph.D. in China.

  • For the general informaion about CSC, please check http://en.csc.edu.cn/ and http://www.csc.edu.cn/Laihua/. All the information is provided as English.
  • Please check http://www.csc.edu.cn/Laihua/noticeen.html and http://www.csc.edu.cn/Laihua/scholarshipen.aspx, for the application information about Chinese Government Scholarships. To apply this, you may need an acceptance letter by the host before you start the official application. Please send me your CV, your transcripts, and your personal letter which introduces yourself and states the reason why you are interested in joining my group.
  • Please check "Chinese Government Scholarship – Chinese University Program" of Zhejiang University 2018. This year, the application deadline is April 20, 2018. To those students from Pakistan: In Section III. 7 instead of language proficiency HSK/IELTS/TOFEL, Pakistani student can submit an official letter which shows that the medium of teaching/examinations is English in their parent universities where they completed Masters degree.
  • Please select the major "Electronic Science and Technology" or "Cyberspace Security" under College of Information Science and Engineering from the list. And please use this template for the acceptance letter to ZJU.

Anorther source is from Zhejiang University. There are other sources that you may find financial oppotunities for your Ph.D.

  • Zhejiang University Scholarship for International Postgraduate Students
  • Doctoral Degress Programs at ZJU
  • Zhejiang University Scholarship—Two-High Doctoral Program
  • Confucius Institute Scholarship
  • International Campus of Zhejiang University, Haining Campus

If you are applying for PostDoc in ZJU (not for Ph.D.) with three years of salary or compersnation, please find the related information here. Please contact Ms. Wang (email: wangjunxia@zju.edu.cn) for more information.
