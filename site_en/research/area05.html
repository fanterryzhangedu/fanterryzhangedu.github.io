Random Delay

Random delay is one type of contermeasures in SCA. The other common one is random masking

  1. An Effcient Method for Random Delay Generation in Embedded Software [PDF]
    The original random delay paper on CHES 2009
  2. Analysis and Improvement of the Random Delay Countermeasure of CHES 2009 [PDF]
    A followup paper published on CHES2010
  3. Cryptanalysis of the CHES 2009/2010 Random Delay Countermeasure [PDF]
    An eprint paper about the previous CHES papers by FX
  4. RAM: Rapid Alignment Method [PDF]
    A paper about the new alignment method, also several wavelet methods
  5. Breaking Cryptographic Implementations Using Deep Learning Techniques [PDF] The very first deep learning paper coping with template attack, published on SPACE2016, keynote talk by Emmanuel Prouff
  6. Convolutional Neural Networks with Data Augmentation Against Jitter-Based Countermeasures Profiling Attacks Without Pre-processing [PDF] A deep learning paper coping with random delays, published on CHES2017
  7. SCATTER : A New Dimension in Side-Channel [PDF]
    a paper about how to do the alignment, 2017, which is related with random delay
  8. Convolutional Neural Networks with Data Augmentation Against Jitter-Based Countermeasures Profiling Attacks Without Pre-processing [PDF] A deep learning paper using CNN on random delay, published on CHES2017
